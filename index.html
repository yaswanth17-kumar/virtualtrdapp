How to use news (instructions)
  • Ask first: is it about the company (results, orders, management) or the whole sector/market?
  • Check the chart: has the price already moved? News is often priced in within minutes.
  • Never trade on one headline. Look for a second source and for the actual numbers.
  • Before acting, write your reason in the Trading Journal (Profile tab) and set your stop-loss first.
